   Architecture selection: Layered Architecture
     - Presentation, Application/Business, and Data/Integration layers
     - Two reasons for the choice:
         1. The platform runs one sequential workflow (recommendation,
            availability, booking, scorecards, history), so a layered
            design keeps the business rules in one place.
         2. It is a single integrated platform with one external
            calendar/meeting integration. That makes layered simpler to
            build, test and deploy than microservices.
     - Security advantage: access control at the presentation and
       application boundary, with the data layer restricting access to
       profiles and scorecards
     - Performance benefit: indexing and caching to meet the 3-second
       response target
     - Comparison with Microservices and Client-Server
     - Component note: the handout's Order Manager and Payment Service
       belong to its coffee-kiosk example. I replaced them with Booking &
       Session Management and Mentor Recommendation & Profile Service.

   UML component diagram
     - Presentation layer: Student Portal, Mentor Portal, Admin Portal
     - Business services layer: Mentor Recommendation & Profile Service,
       Booking & Session Management, Scorecard & Feedback Service
     - External integration layer: Calendar/Meeting Service,
       Notification Service
     - Data layer: User / Session Database
